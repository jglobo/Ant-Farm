GOAL

FIGHTING

Everyone bites once a second. Workers bite back for 1, soldiers for 3, the queen for 5. Enemies bite for 2.
Press DEFEND when raiders come: every ant drops its work and rallies round the queen, then goes back to work once the raid is over.

THREATS

Rain floods the shafts — builders dam it. Sandstorms bury tunnels. Injured ants eat food to heal.
